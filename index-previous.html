The Sherpa Route

How we get you to your score.

Every student follows the same four stages, at their own pace.

  1. Stage 1

    Map

    Know where you stand.

    A short diagnostic on day one and a personal roadmap showing where your preparation should be before every SAC.

  2. Stage 2

    Climb

    Learn it in small groups.

    Weekly classes with a daily tracker, resource packs and exemplars for every section, so you can see what a top response looks like.

  3. Stage 3

    Prove

    Practise under real conditions.

    Marked practice SACs and full SAC walkthroughs. English students are marked a week out, and again three days out.

  4. Stage 4

    Review

    Keep the plan honest.

    A written report for parents at week four, and a plan that changes the moment something stops working.

FAQ

Questions parents ask.

Can we try Sherpa before enrolling?

Yes. You can book a free fit call, a free trial class or a free diagnostic. All three are free, with no obligation to enrol.

What happens on the fit call?

It takes about 15 minutes. We ask where the student is now and what has gone wrong, name the specific gap, and recommend one plan. You leave knowing the cost and the first class date.

What is the minimum commitment?

One 5-week block. At week four you get a written report and the option to move to a term or full year.

What do we receive after enrolling?

A receipt and welcome email with class times and where to attend, plus access to the class group chat and resource drive. Your tutor also sets a short diagnostic task for day one.

What if a student misses a class?

If a student misses two classes, we call to find out why and help them catch up.

Do you offer discounts?

Yes. There are discounts for extra subjects and siblings, and a credit for both sides when you refer a friend. The pricing page has the details.
