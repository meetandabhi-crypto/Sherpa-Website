How we teach

The Sherpa Way.

Small groups for the energy. One-on-one help for the gaps. A step-by-step framework for every part of the course. And a tutor who's a message away, any time.

I

Small groups, with one-on-one help

Learn alongside a few others who are working just as hard, then get one-on-one help from your guide on exactly the parts you're stuck on. Nobody hides at the back.

II

A framework for every step

Every part of the study design is broken into clear, step-by-step frameworks: how to plan an essay, attack a data question or lay out a ledger. You always know the next move.

III

Real accountability

Your guide knows your goals, your SAC dates and your weak spots, and checks in on all of them. If you start slipping, we notice early and pull you back on track.

Base camp

Find your footing

A free trial class and a quick chat about where you're at: your school's SAC calendar, your current marks and your target score. We work out where you're starting from.

Camp one

Build the foundations

Each week's class teaches content ahead of school, so lessons at school feel like revision. Clear notes and worked examples sit behind every concept.

Camp two

Train for the conditions

Exam-style questions, SAC preparation and the marking habits examiners reward. We drill the skills that separate a 40 from a 45+.

Camp three

Acclimatise under pressure

Timed practice exams, targeted feedback and strategy for the hardest questions. By October, the exam room should feel familiar.

FAQ

Questions before the climb.

Can't find your answer? Send us a message.

Who is Sherpa Education for?

Year 12 students taking Units 3/4 VCE English, VCE Psychology, VCE General Mathematics or VCE Accounting, whether you're chasing a 30+ or a 45+.

How much does it cost?

$80 per week, per subject. The price is the same for every subject, and class resources are included.

Who will actually teach my child?

Our head tutors. Abhinandan Chandran (ATAR 99.05) teaches VCE English and VCE Psychology. Meet Shah (ATAR 99.65) teaches VCE General Maths and VCE Accounting.

How big are the classes?

Small by design. Groups are kept small enough that every student can ask questions and get feedback in every class, with one-on-one help on top for whatever you need.

Can I contact my tutor between classes?

Yes, any time. Every student can message their head tutor 24/7 with questions, working to check or a paragraph to look over. You won't have to wait until next week's class.

How do you keep students accountable?

Your guide knows your goals and your school's SAC calendar, and checks in on homework and preparation along the way. If something slips, we notice early and help you get back on track.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Your first class is free, with no payment details and no obligation. If it's not the right fit, you walk away.

Can I join partway through the year?

Yes. Lots of students join mid-year. We'll help you catch up on anything you've missed so you're on the same route as the group.

Do you tutor Year 11 or other subjects?

Right now we focus only on Units 3/4 in our four subjects. That focus is how we keep quality high. Get in touch if you'd like to hear when we expand.
